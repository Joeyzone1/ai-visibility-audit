Harbour Dental Care

Harbour Dental Care has treated families on the south quay since 1998. We offer general dentistry, hygiene appointments, and emergency cover on weekends.

Services

Check ups, cleaning, whitening, crowns, and same day repairs. Appointments run from eight in the morning until six in the evening on weekdays.

Getting here

Two minutes from the quay bus stop, with parking behind the building.
